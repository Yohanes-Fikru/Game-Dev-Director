# Production

Production begins when the team can repeatedly turn known inputs into playable
content at the intended quality. It is not permission to stop experimenting.

Organize work around playable milestones. Identify the content pipeline,
ownership, dependencies, throughput bottleneck, quality bar, technical budgets,
and integration cadence. Prefer improving a proven pipeline over adding parallel
systems or tooling.

For each milestone:

- define the player-visible outcome and integrated acceptance evidence;
- expose critical-path dependencies and irreversible decisions;
- reserve capacity for integration, bugs, performance, and playtesting;
- track content remaining with honest confidence, not optimistic completion;
- cut or descale work when throughput cannot meet the target.

Escalate to fuller process only when team size, platform requirements, or
coordination cost demands it. Small teams do not need simulated departments or
ceremonies.
